Example Release 1.0 lets a reader pin a note, browse saved releases and recover a draft.

Pin a note

If you keep returning to a note, click Pin to keep it at the top of your list.

Browse releases

Choose a product, then open a release to see what changed.

Recover a draft

A saved draft stays available when you close the editor and return later.

Reuse a link

Copy a release link to share the original source with a teammate.
